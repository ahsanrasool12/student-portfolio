Joining a Club Made Easy

  1. Explore: Visit the Clubs page and choose a club that matches your interests.
  2. Apply: Collect the membership form from the Student Affairs office and submit it.
  3. Meet the Team: Come to the next club session and introduce yourself to the committee.
  4. Get Involved: Join club activities, help organise events and learn new skills.

Meeting Schedule

Clubs usually hold weekly sessions. Common timing: Fridays, 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

Member Guidelines

  • Treat every member with respect.
  • Be present at club sessions on time.
  • Follow the university rules and your club's code of conduct.
